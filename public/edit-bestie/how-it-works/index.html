Questions

Do I need to be technical?

No. You click four buttons once to open your folder in the Claude app, then you talk to it in plain words. No coding, no terminal, no editing app.

Will it look like me, or like everyone else?

Like you. At setup it learns your look, your colour and the words it must spell right. Show it a reel you love and it builds your own look from it.

Does it touch anything else on my Mac or phone?

No. Claude only uses the footage you put in your video's VO and B-roll folders. Your Photos, other files and phone stay untouched.

Do you see my videos?

No. Your clips are edited on your own Mac and never uploaded to us.

Can I film in Chinese, Thai or another language?

Yes. Tell Claude your language at setup and your captions come out in it.

Does it work on Windows?

Not yet. It's Mac only for now.

What if I get stuck?

Tell Claude "fix it". Still stuck? DM STUCK to @the.ai.bestie and we'll get you running.
